### Version 1.0.0 — Capsule VN (Official Initial Release)
- **Official Capsule VN Identity**: Dynamic Island experience for Android completely localized and enhanced with cutting-edge features.
- **Smart 1-Tap OTP Copy & Autofill**: Automatically detects 4–8 digit verification codes from SMS, banking apps and messengers with auto-copy and 1-tap screen autofill.
- **Headset Voice Announcer (TTS)**: Reads incoming messages and caller names aloud in natural Vietnamese when headphones are connected, with automatic music ducking.
- **Capsule Edge LED Lighting**: 360° RGB Rainbow, Cyber Neon, Breathing Gold, or app-adaptive glowing border around the capsule for unread notifications, charging, and events.
- **Gaming Mode & Ecosystem Integration**: Compact non-intrusive game pills, live **Gaming HUD (°C & RAM)**, **Samsung S Pen**, **Galaxy Buds/Watch**, **Super Fast Charging**, and **DeX / Smart View**.
- **3D iOS Liquid Glass & Separate Message / Call Card Sizing**: Customize vertical offset, width, and height independently for message and call cards with live preview.
